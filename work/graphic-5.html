Natural Coconut Water Brand

Challenge: The tropical beverage category is over-saturated with neon blue and green clichés. The task was to cut through the visual noise by highlighting the purity of the raw ingredient.

Solution: A radical White and Brown palette, the actual colours of a coconut "unfiltered honesty" on the shelf. The circular typographic layout mirrors the coconut's physical form, ensuring instant category recognition through minimalist geometry.
